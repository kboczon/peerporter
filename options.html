PeerPorter

Adds an PT button to every YouTube thumbnail. One click queues that video into your PeerTube channel. Your password is used once to get OAuth tokens; after that the extension refreshes them on its own (PeerTube refresh tokens last 28 days and are renewed on each use).

1. Connect to your PeerTube instance

Grey placeholder text is not a value - type your own hostname here, for example youtube.wtools.us.

PeerTube needs its OAuth client pair, so the first call reads /api/v1/oauth-clients/local, then the token comes from /api/v1/users/token.

3. Housekeeping

Queued videos are remembered for 30 days so a second click cannot queue the same video twice. Logging out keeps the channel choice; it only removes the tokens.
